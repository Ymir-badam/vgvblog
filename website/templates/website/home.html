About the Firm

Building business confidence through financial excellence.

Founded after 15 years of extensive professional experience, VGV Konsultancy was established to provide dependable, high-quality financial, taxation, and compliance services to businesses across industries.

Our expertise spans direct and indirect taxation, GST compliance, Income Tax matters, accounting, bookkeeping, payroll management, ROC compliance, internal auditing, financial reporting, and statutory compliance. We help businesses simplify regulatory obligations, strengthen financial controls, and make informed decisions.

"We believe in building long-term client relationships through integrity, professionalism, and personalized service."

Alongside VGV Konsultancy, our founder also serves as an Internal Auditor at Glonebile Shipping India Pvt. Ltd., bringing practical experience in corporate governance, internal controls, risk management, and operational efficiency.
